Derived Frameworks

NIST SP 800-171 (CUI), NIST SP 800-172 (Enhanced Security Requirements) and IEC 62443-4-2 each derive their requirements from NIST 800-53 controls rather than listing rules directly. This page resolves each requirement against the current product's 800-53 controls to show real rule coverage and status, independent of the requirement's own authored status. Frameworks that a product does not ship are omitted.
